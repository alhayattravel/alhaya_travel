Articles & Travel Guides Section

Your smart guide to travel at the lowest cost and best experience

✈️ Smart Travel Art: How to travel the world on a budget

Many believe luxury travel requires a huge budget, but smart planning is more important than money. Best days to book flights are Tuesday and Wednesday. Avoid weekends, and enable price alerts through our website. Look for budget apartments on city outskirts connected to the metro to save 40% of the cost.

🕵️‍♂️ How does the ticket booking system fool you?

Airlines rely on cookies to track your interest. Each time you repeat a search, the system inflates the price. Solution? Use Incognito mode. Combined stops trick: sometimes a flight from Riyadh to London with a stop in Istanbul is cheaper than Riyadh to Istanbul directly.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Traveling with kids stress-free

Look for hotels offering free kids' stay and small kitchens. Don't visit 5 landmarks in one day with kids; keep your schedule to one morning activity then rest.

🗺️ Hidden Gems

Tbilisi (Georgia) and Baku (Azerbaijan) are among the cheapest destinations with hotel prices starting at $30, easy visa, and low cost of living.

🎒 Solo Travel

Plan only the first 48 hours, stay in top-rated Hostels to meet travelers, and download Google Maps offline.

🆘 What to do if your passport is lost?

Head to the nearest police station to file a report, then contact your embassy. Always keep a digital copy of your passport in your email.

🌱 Sustainable Travel

Use a refillable bottle, rely on bikes and metro, and shop local markets to save money and protect the environment.

🛡️ Do you need travel insurance?

Insurance policy covers astronomical medical costs in US and Europe, lost baggage compensation, and sudden cancellation refunds.

🏨 Hidden Hotel Booking Guide

Always book with free cancellation option. If price drops, cancel and rebook. Contact the hotel directly after booking to get a free upgrade.

💳 How to protect your money while traveling?

Inform your bank before traveling, withdraw from official ATMs, and choose to pay in local currency.

🧳 How to pack your bag like an expert

Roll clothes into cylinders instead of folding to save 40% space. Rule of 5-4-3-2-1: 5 socks, 4 tops, 3 bottoms, 2 shoes, 1 main item.

📸 Professional phone photos

Enable Grid and Rule of Thirds, use the golden hour after sunrise and before sunset.

⏰ Overcoming Jet Lag

Set your watch immediately upon boarding to destination time, expose yourself to natural sunlight.

💱 Avoiding currency exchange loss

Don't exchange much at the airport. At ATM choose local currency, not your home country currency.

⚠️ 5 Tourist traps that steal your money

Don't take unlicensed taxis outside arrival halls; use official parking or transport apps. At restaurants ask if appetizers are free.

🌆 Istanbul - Between East and West

Economical, historically rich, Bosphorus ferry for a small fee, Sultanahmet, Spice Bazaar, Galata Tower at sunset.

🌴 Cairo - Open Air Museum

Nile dinner at sunset, Khan el-Khalili, Pyramids easily via our site and budget car rentals.

🕌 Dubai - From Dubai Creek to the Future

Start with wooden Abra ferry for dirhams, spice and gold souks in Deira, then Dubai Marina, Burj Khalifa.

🏔️ Salalah - Autumn Paradise in the Desert

Between July and September Dhofar mountains turn green with waterfalls and fog, ideal for families.

🏔️ Budget Switzerland

Stay in Interlaken or border villages, use regional transport passes instead of individual tickets.

🏝️ Budget Maldives

Head to local islands like Maafushi instead of isolated resorts, same turquoise water and amazing hotel prices.

🏺 Petra - Jordan

Walking through the Siq to the Treasury, with Bedouin camps in Wadi Rum and budget hotels via our platform.
